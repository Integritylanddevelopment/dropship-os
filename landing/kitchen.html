How to use it

  1. Wet the sponge and add a drop of dish soap — a little goes far with an absorbent set.
  2. Scrub dishes and cookware with the textured side; wipe counters and spills with the soft side.
  3. Rinse thoroughly under running water until it runs clean.
  4. Squeeze out and stand it to air-dry between uses. Replace on a sensible schedule like any kitchen cloth.

Details & care

  • Rinse well and air-dry after each use.
  • Refresh periodically per normal sponge hygiene practice.
  • Avoid using on hot surfaces straight off the burner.
  • Retire and replace on a regular schedule as with any dish sponge.
