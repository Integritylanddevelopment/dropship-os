About the wash nozzle

A full pressure washer is a great tool — and a purchase, a storage problem, a setup ritual and a maintenance schedule. For most weekend jobs, what you actually need is simpler: take the hose you already own and make the water work harder. That is exactly what the High-Pressure Wash Nozzle does.

The nozzle concentrates your hose's ordinary flow into a focused, forceful stream. Dialed tight, it blasts caked dirt off wheel wells, mud off boots, algae film off siding and gunk out of bin corners. Opened up, it becomes a wide, fast rinse for soap suds, patio furniture and dusty steps.

Car day is where it earns its keep. Rinse the body with the wider pattern, then tighten the stream for wheels, arches and rocker panels where road grime cakes on. The adjustability means you control the intensity — strong where you need bite, gentle where you're rinsing paint.

Because it is hand-sized, there is no machine to drag out, no cord, no motor noise, and nothing to winterize. It lives on a shelf by the spigot, connects to a standard garden hose, and turns "I should really clean that" into a ten-minute job you'll actually do.

How to use it

  1. Attach the nozzle to a standard garden hose and turn the water on fully.
  2. Start with a wider spray to wet the surface and carry away loose dirt.
  3. Tighten the stream over stubborn grime — wheels, corners, crevices — letting the focused jet do the scrubbing.
  4. Finish with a wide rinse, shut off the water, and hang the nozzle by the spigot for next time.

Details & care

  • Drain after use and store indoors over winter to prevent freeze damage.
  • Keep the focused stream away from people, pets, window seals and fragile screens.
  • Test on a small area first when cleaning painted or delicate surfaces.
  • Check the hose washer seal seasonally for drip-free connection.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same as a pressure washer?

No — it is a high-performance nozzle that focuses your garden hose's existing flow. It delivers a much stronger working stream than an open hose without the cost, bulk or maintenance of a machine.

Will it damage car paint?

Used sensibly, no. Rinse paint with the wider pattern and reserve the tight stream for wheels, arches and lower panels — the same practice used at any hand car wash.

Does water pressure at my house matter?

Results scale with your home's water pressure. The nozzle concentrates whatever flow your hose delivers into a far more effective cleaning stream.
