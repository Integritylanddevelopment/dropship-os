How to use it

  1. Clear a workspace and lay out the ten monster cards so your maker can pick their first victim — er, creation.
  2. Follow the card's steps to assemble the monster's body, then mix and match features to give it personality.
  3. Decorate: the sillier the eyes, teeth and colors, the better. There is no wrong monster.
  4. Display the finished crew on a shelf, gift them to friends, or start a monster battle — creator's choice.

Details & care

  • Recommended for children in the craft-scissors age range; supervise younger makers.
  • Small parts — keep away from toddlers and pets.
  • Store leftover pieces in the box for the next session.
